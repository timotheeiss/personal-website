## Featured projects

### Newgrain

Newgrain is a film-photography platform Timothee has been building for more than four years. He took it from prototype to a live product, launched native iOS and web apps, and grew it to more than 10,000 users, 45,000 posts, 350 App Store reviews with a 4.8-star rating, and hundreds of paying subscribers. The team uses React, Swift, Firebase, and AWS. Website: https://newgrain.app.

### Hivemind

Hivemind is an internal semantic search engine Timothee built at Entrepreneurs First. Investors could discover portfolio teams and founders by searching ideas, industries, or technologies in natural language. It launched in mid-2025 as the company's first internal AI tool. Its stack included React, Vertex AI, BigQuery, and the OpenAI API.

### SemTag

SemTag is an end-to-end testing agent for graphical user-interface applications. It aims to use 50% fewer tokens than regular browser agents. It grew from Timothee's master's thesis on optimising AI browsing agents for end-to-end GUI testing and is still in development. It uses Python, React, and the Agents SDK.
